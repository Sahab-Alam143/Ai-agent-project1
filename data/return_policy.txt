SHOPSMART - RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

1. RETURN ELIGIBILITY

Customers can return eligible products within 7 days of receiving their order.
Products must be unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging.

2. NON-RETURNABLE PRODUCTS

The following products cannot be returned:
- Personal care products.
- Innerwear and hygiene products.
- Products marked as non-returnable.
- Products damaged by the customer.

3. DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE PRODUCTS

Customers must report damaged or defective products within 48 hours of delivery.
Photographs or videos may be required as proof.

4. RETURN PROCESS

Customers must provide their Order ID and reason for return.
The store will verify the request before approving or rejecting it.

5. REFUND POLICY

Approved refunds will be initiated within 5-7 business days
after the returned product passes inspection.

6. EXCHANGE POLICY

Customers can request an exchange within 7 days of delivery,
subject to product availability and eligibility.

7. RETURN SHIPPING CHARGES

The store covers return shipping charges for damaged or defective products.
For other eligible returns, customers are responsible for return shipping.

8. REFUND METHOD

Refunds are generally issued through the original payment method.
For cash-on-delivery orders, an approved alternative payment method
may be used.

9. ORDER CANCELLATION

Customers can request cancellation before the order is shipped.
Once shipped, cancellation may not be available.

10. CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers can contact the store's customer support team
for return-related assistance.
