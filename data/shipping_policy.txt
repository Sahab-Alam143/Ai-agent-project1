
SHOPSMART - SHIPPING AND DELIVERY POLICY

1. DELIVERY TIME

Standard delivery usually takes 3-7 business days.
Delivery time depends on the customer's delivery location.

2. SHIPPING CHARGES

Orders above Rs. 999 qualify for free standard shipping.
Orders below Rs. 999 have a standard shipping fee of Rs. 49.

3. ORDER PROCESSING

Orders are generally processed within 1-2 business days
after successful order confirmation.

4. ORDER TRACKING

Customers can track their orders using their Order ID.
The order tracking system displays the latest available status.

5. DELIVERY DELAYS

Delivery may be delayed due to weather conditions,
transportation issues, public holidays, or other unexpected events.

6. FAILED DELIVERY

If delivery fails because the customer is unavailable,
the delivery partner may attempt another delivery.

7. CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers can contact the store's customer support team
for shipping and delivery-related assistance.
